03 / METHODS

Experimental protocol

Frozen checkpoint; matched cameras, goal and control timing.

01

Object count

N ∈ {2, 3, 4}; physical removal of absent objects, with retained dimensions and materials.

02

Spatial conditions

Nominal; ±40 mm jitter; cluster (≥20 mm clearance); one-sided; free source-region placement.

03

Success criterion

All N objects in a supported tower, without hand contact for 10 controls, within 1,795 controls.

04 / LIMITATIONS

Scope of inference
