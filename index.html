03 / EXPERIMENT

Change the scene.
Keep the policy frozen.

A controlled first look at object-count and spatial generalization in box stacking.

01

Vary the inventory

Instantiate exactly 2, 3 or 4 physical objects. Removed objects are absent from geometry and scoring. Retain each object’s original size, height and material.

02

Vary the starting layout

Cross object count with five layouts: nominal anchors, ±40 mm jitter, a cluster with at least 20 mm surface clearance, one-sided placement and a free source-region layout.

03

Preserve the task

Keep the released cameras, goal and controller timing. Require every physical object in a supported tower, released for 10 controls, within the same 1,795-control budget.
